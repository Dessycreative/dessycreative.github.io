Play

Find the bad join

A report total that doesn't tie out to the ledger. Can you spot why?

Two orders each have two deliveries. Joining orders to deliveries before summing counts those orders twice, so the report shows €720 against a ledger of €400. Collapse deliveries to one row per order first and the totals are reconciled.
